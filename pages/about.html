Legacy OCR Dilemma

Fragmented Text & Lost Geometry

Traditional OCR engines treat documents as flat text streams—jumbling multi-column spreadsheets, discarding mathematical symbols, and missing small captions or low-contrast footnotes.

Cloud-based OCR APIs routinely harvest user documents to train external LLMs, compromising corporate secrets, medical records, and legal intellectual property.

The HorizonOCR Paradigm

Structured Vision & Absolute Privacy

HorizonOCR unifies deep-learning DBNet text segmentation, PP-OCRv3 recognition, and PyMuPDF layout heuristics to reconstruct perfect GitHub-Flavored Markdown tables and vector LaTeX equations.

Every document is processed in a zero-leak sandbox. Images are stored in encrypted SQLite BLOBs bound strictly to your authenticated session ID.

Core Innovation Pillars

Engineered for Extreme Document Complexity

Dual-Pass Vision Detection
Combines DBNet deep learning probability heatmaps with adaptive morphological filters to guarantee zero missed text contours.
Micro-Text Super-Resolution
Bicubic interpolation dynamically scales small text crops (6pt–8pt footnotes, serial codes, nano-labels) to 32px+ height for optimal character recognition.
Structural Table Recovery

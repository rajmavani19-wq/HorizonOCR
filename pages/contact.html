Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

What document formats and file size limits are supported?
HorizonOCR natively supports multi-page PDF documents (up to 500 pages per file) and high-resolution PNG image scans. The maximum upload file size limit is 50MB per file.
How does HorizonOCR ensure 100% document privacy?
All documents and extracted graphics are bound strictly to your authenticated user_id. Temporary rasterization files are immediately purged from server memory upon OCR completion. Your documents and extracted data are never sold or used to train third-party AI models.
How does the micro-text super-resolution engine work?
Our multi-scale DBNet vision detector identifies even faint text contours. Small bounding boxes (such as 6pt–8pt footnotes, serial codes, or subscripts) are scaled with Bicubic interpolation to a normalized height of 32px+ before recognition to guarantee maximum accuracy.
Can HorizonOCR be deployed in a private air-gapped or on-premises environment?
Yes. HorizonOCR is container-ready (Docker / Gunicorn / SQLite WAL) and can run on air-gapped CPU or NVIDIA CUDA GPU servers with zero external internet dependencies. Reach out via the contact form for custom deployment guidance.
